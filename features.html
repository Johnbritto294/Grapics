Handover

The handover is part of the design.

A beautiful file that nobody can open is not finished. Every project ends with a tidy kit: named layers, linked assets, export presets and a short guide to how the system works.

  • Native Illustrator, InDesign and Figma files
  • Fonts, licences and colour values documented
  • Print and screen export presets
  • A one-page guide for your team
Ask for a sample kit
Print room

Colour is a physical decision.

A hex code is a starting point. We match ink to stock, check the proof under the press lights and sign off the sheet that will actually run. That is why the yellow on your box matches the yellow on your van.

  • Pantone and CMYK builds checked on press
  • Proofs signed before the full run
  • Finish samples kept in the studio library
  • Print partner briefed in person or on a call
